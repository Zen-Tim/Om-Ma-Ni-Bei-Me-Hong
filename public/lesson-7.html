Mmm: releasing suffering is compassion

The third sound of Om. Mmm means releasing suffering, first your own and then everyone's. Sifu shows that releasing is compassion, how ego hides inside compassion, how the mantra helps, and how releasing leads to Nirvana.

Summary

The third sound of Om is Mmm. Mmm means releasing suffering: being free from suffering and from reincarnation, and awakening.

  • Mmm = releasing: Oh, part one, is understanding where suffering comes from. Mmm, part two, brings that power out to help yourself, then every life.
  • Mmm = compassion: releasing your own suffering is compassion to yourself. Helping others release theirs is compassion to others.
  • Ego in compassion: most compassion still has ego in it. True, spiritual compassion has five conditions.
  • Two ways the mantra helps: you learn the wisdom yourself, and the sound connects you to awakened consciousness, which helps you. The main cause is still you.
  • Worry: a blind habit. It starts as a fair warning and grows into fear that takes over life. Seen the right way, worry is a lesson.
  • The joy of helping others: a very deep, very pure joy. Before you reach it, there is a big challenge.

Releasing starts at the temporary level: mind, psychology and body. When all three are free, the Mmm of Om appears, and it leads to Nirvana, the fourth sound.

Mmm = releasing: what it means

  • Mmm means releasing suffering. You become free from suffering, free from reincarnation, and you awaken.
  • To release suffering, you understand how its roots can be taken out.
  • Om has two parts. Part one is Oh, the understanding. Part two is Mmm: bring the power of part one out to help everyone.
  • First you release yourself. When that works, you use the power to help every life. That is the Buddhist idea of true compassion.
Mmm is wisdom-style compassion, towards yourself and towards every life.

Lesson 6 said Mmm is the trained ability to release suffering. See Understanding is not ability. Lesson 7 adds that this ability is compassion.

Mmm = compassion: the ego kinds

Compassion comes in many kinds. Most of them still carry ego.

Feel-good compassion

You help because you want to feel good about yourself, or you want people to know you are a good person.

Blind compassion

You want to help others, but you do not yet know how to help yourself.

Self-pity compassion

Deep down, you are afraid of suffering yourself. Someone else's suffering feels related to you, so you want to help them. Really, you want to help yourself.

Sifu's example: the student and his sister

  • A student wanted to help his sister. She was depressed, overweight and not healthy.
  • Sifu told him to start with himself: learn to be free from negative thoughts and negative psychology, and learn to be strong and healthy.
  • He wanted to help her because she was his sister. But he did not know how, and he felt he was drowning in the wish to help.
  • Sifu called it an emotional ego game for himself, not real help. The student was shocked.

Sifu: we all have this ego-style compassion.

Mmm = compassion: the five conditions of true compassion

Tim recalled a checklist from years ago. Sifu agreed with all five.

  1. You have the ability to help.
  2. It is your own free will to help.
  3. It is their free will to choose you: they ask for your help.
  4. You enjoy helping.
  5. The help goes in a wise, positive, spiritual direction.

What is reincarnation?

  • Reincarnation is blind birth, again and again, with no way out of it.
  • You keep being reborn the blind way, until you truly have the freedom to control your own birth and death.
  • Reincarnation is like gambling, the bad kind: blind, with no control. You could be born into unlimited possibilities with unlimited suffering. That is scary.
  • But reincarnation also has a positive side. It is like a nightmare. If you are dreaming a nightmare, you can still wake up.
Since we are already blind in reincarnation, the wisdom to wake up is surely there too, waiting for us to find it.

Two ways Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong helps you awaken

Direct: the wisdom

You learn the wisdom yourself. That is why learning the meaning of the mantra matters.

Connection: the sound

The sound of the mantra is a channel, a wave, that connects you to Buddha, Bodhisattva and awakened consciousness. They support you to awaken.

The mantra does both. Without the meaning, you miss the foundation.

Help even without the meaning

  • If you are sincere, you still get the second kind of help. Say the mantra with respect and appreciation, even without understanding it, and you still connect to Buddha and Bodhisattva.
  • Sifu asked GD why. GD: you are creating a cause. You are humble, so you are letting go of your ego, or learning to. That lets a higher level of consciousness help you. And you are asking for help.
  • Sifu: you prepare the cause, and life is cause and effect.
  • Buddha and Bodhisattva always want to help, not only you but every life, out of all blindness and suffering. They naturally want to share that wisdom.
  • But they cannot help you, or control your life, if you do not ask sincerely from your heart.
If you understand the meaning as well, it is a double force. You help yourself, and they help you. That is even faster.

Lesson 2 gave the same two conditions: understand the meaning and a sincere heart.

How the mantra releases suffering

Tim's summary: you release suffering through wisdom and understanding. You are the main cause. You develop it as an ability you can share, to help yourself and other lives. Perfect that ability, and you can help unlimited lives. Sifu agreed.

  1. The sound connects you to Buddha and Bodhisattva. They change your karma, and guide you to find someone who can teach you.
  2. The meaning gathers Dharma nutrients. Your wisdom starts to grow. You learn to release your own suffering, then others' suffering.
  • The main cause is still yourself, but it grows two ways. Your wisdom grows, and the connection and protection from Buddha and Bodhisattva turns your karma in a spiritual direction, so you get the chance to learn.
  • Tim: without the mantra's connection, it would only be ourselves trying to understand. Through awakened consciousness, compassion helps guide our life, and guides others into our life. Once our level is high enough, it opens our heart to share.

Suffering from changing: birth, aging, illness, death

When we understand the meaning, we start to see that life is blind birth and blind death, again and again.

StageWhy it is suffering
BirthA blind birth, without knowing how or why.
AgingAging is change. Not only the body changes. Your whole situation changes, and you face new challenges.
IllnessDirect suffering: physical, mental and psychological.
DeathWhen the suffering is so strong that the body breaks down completely, fear appears. We have to face the complete unknown.
  • We never think it is important to see through this and be free from it.
  • With Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong, we understand that life uses suffering to teach us, and that there is an answer.
  • So we become curious to learn about life, not only to search for outside, material things.
  • Holding on to outside things is a blind habit. It is dangerous, because everyone still faces a blind death and loses everything.
  • Many people give their whole life and their health for outside things. That is the wrong way, and it is why aging, illness and death come.
The mantra changes our intention. It purifies our heart, so we learn to live healthy and become wise.

An example: worry

How does a simple warning become a life of worry?

GD recalled last week's example: you rush, you are not aware, and you bang your elbow, so next time you do not rush. Tim wanted an example that goes deeper. GD suggested worry, and Sifu explained it.

  1. Pain and suffering
  2. You get scared
  3. A warning
  4. Fear
  5. Worry is all that is left
  • Worry is a very tricky system. Our own heart creates it.
  • The warning gets passed down. Parents who suffered and made mistakes pass their worry on to us. Then we make our own mistakes growing up, and we get our own warnings.
  • At first it is only a warning: "I have to be careful." Being careful is not wrong, if you stay calm and care in a peaceful way.
  • Then it becomes fear. You always feel, "I am going to do it wrong." You get so stressed you cannot enjoy life. That is the blind habit of worry.
  • It goes deeper. If you do not worry, you feel nothing. Worry becomes the only thing left, and life is real suffering. We get lost in the trick.
  • Worry shows in your wrinkles: even when you smile, you are suffering. It shows in your body language. You no longer move like a child: not flexible, not carefree. No wisdom, no curiosity, no joy.
  • "I worry I am going to worry later. I don't worry enough now." It started as something simple. Now it is very powerful.

Worry is a lesson

  • Worry is nothing bad. We can read it the right way and start to understand it.
  • We learn to clean it up, and not get lost in it. We earn wisdom from it.
  • Because we are willing to help ourselves feel free and enjoy life, we can help others too.
We enjoy life, and we help other people enjoy life. That is the meaning of life.

Joy is a gift you create

  • Life is meant to be enjoyed, but joy does not come as a gift forever.
  • When you are young, you get a taste of the gift. Life is a lot of fun, but not forever.
  • You have to learn how to develop it. You have to create joy. Then you can help others enjoy life.
  • That is double joy: a gift from your own creation.

The joy of helping others

  • GD: releasing suffering for yourself is one thing. Helping another person release their suffering, and have a lot more joy in their life, is another level of enjoying life. You have already helped yourself, so helping them creates a different kind of joy. It is another layer.
  • Sifu: it is a very deep, very pure joy. But before you reach that joy, there is a big challenge.
  • GD agreed: it is not easy.

Releasing: temporary and eternal

Compassion, towards yourself and towards others, has two types: temporary and eternal. Temporary releasing has three parts.

  1. Mind: free from worry.
  2. Psychology: do not get trapped in stress. Do not follow negative emotion of any kind, or moody, negative feelings. This is very, very difficult.
  3. Body: make the body powerful. Curl the stomach to charge up the energy. Bring that charge up into a proud, open chest and open shoulders. The whole body connects as one piece, like a dragon twisting, with the breath. This takes the body out of the blindness of tension and suffering, into a powerful, healthy human body.

When you have the power to be free in all three, the Mmm of Om appears.

  1. Free in mind, psychology and body
  2. Mmm appears
  3. You enjoy life and help others
  4. Spiritual karma appears
  5. The heart is completely content
  6. Nirvana, the silence

Lesson 6 gave the same body work through Bodhidharma's Yi Jin Jing and Zhang San Feng's tai chi. Lesson 5 called the two types the temporary fix and the permanent fix.

A first look at Nirvana: the fourth sound

  • Nirvana is when your consciousness is completely free, no longer limited by the body.
  • You can be aware of any space and any time. You are aware that the true essence of life is already perfect.
  • Our true essence has the ability to create spiritual life and spiritual joy.
  • Because you make yourself free from blindness, you go back to your essence. There you find your essence is already perfect. That is natural Nirvana.

Nirvana becomes unlimited

  • Your own perfect, joyful essence creates spirit, light, joy, wisdom and compassion.
  • Then you help others reach Nirvana too. When they become free from suffering and reincarnation, they go back to their own essence, and experience Nirvana from their own essence.
  • You connect to their journey. You share it, and you are aware of it. Your awareness is no longer limited to your own perfect essence. It connects to the perfect essence of every life you help awaken.
The more you connect and the more you help, the more unlimited it becomes. That is unlimited joy through eternal wisdom.

Sifu's closing wish: may everyone be free from blindness, find their perfect essence, and go into unlimited joy.
