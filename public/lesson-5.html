What is understanding?

Summary

The second sound of Om is Oh. Oh means understanding. Lesson 4 explained Argh, suffering. Lesson 5 explains how to understand suffering.

  • Oh = understanding has two jobs: understand where suffering comes from, and understand how to make suffering go away.
  • Oh = understanding, two kinds of fix: a temporary fix treats the symptom. A permanent fix removes the root cause.
  • Oh = understanding, three kinds of suffering today: mental suffering is worry. Psychological suffering is fear, which we feel as stress. Physical suffering is pain and tension. The three form a circle, and worry and stress are stored in the body.
  • Oh = understanding, fear: all fear comes from one fear, the fear of death. Fear of death is one of the four devils.
  • Oh = understanding, two sides of the fix: the passive side clears out the bad. The active side builds the good. Doing both is best.

Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong has power for all three kinds of suffering. That is why modern people need the mantra. How the mantra works on them comes in a later class.

Recap of Lesson 4: suffering is an effect, and every effect comes from a cause. Our own blind action creates the cause.

Why Sifu adds other views to Buddhism

  • Buddhism speaks from a complete, high view of life. Its words can be sharp, deep and dry, far from daily life.
  • Dry words can turn into blind belief.
  • So Sifu adds other views to make the teaching concrete: Daoism, Chinese medicine, Western ideas, martial arts. The main line stays with Buddhism.
  • An idea can sound perfect and still not help. The teaching has to improve our consciousness in real life.

Oh = understanding: two jobs

  1. Understand what causes suffering, and where suffering comes from.
  2. Understand how to make suffering go away.

Understanding the cause alone does not free you from suffering. You need the second job too.

Oh = understanding: temporary fix and permanent fix

Temporary fix

Treat the symptom. Example: take a painkiller for a headache. The painkiller works now, but the headache can come back.

Permanent fix

Find the root cause, so the suffering never comes back.

  • Chinese medicine heals both the cause and the symptom. Ending suffering works the same way.
  • We need both. Sometimes we need an answer right now. For the long term, go deep so the suffering never happens again. Seeing the same suffering come back again and again is no fun.
  • People get stuck in two ways. Some are stuck and want an answer now. Others know a fix, but the suffering keeps coming back, and they are tired of it.
Oh means the wisdom to answer both.

Oh = understanding: the fix must be stronger than the suffering

  • This sound is even deeper than the first. Sifu says you could write 10 books on it.
  • If suffering is a 5, you need wisdom of 10, or even 50. The fix must be stronger than the suffering.
  • A temporary fix needs a lot of wisdom. A permanent fix needs wisdom plus a training system.

Oh = understanding: three kinds of suffering today

Mental

Worry.

Psychological

Fear. We feel that fear as stress: depression, upset, moody, any negative feeling.

Physical

Pain and tension.

Money, job and relationship problems all fit inside mental and psychological suffering.

Oh = understanding: stress is fear

  • We feel the stress, but we do not know what the stress is. Example: you feel uncomfortable, but you do not know why.
  • Buddhism teaches that this stress is fear, in many different forms.
Seeing that the stress is fear is itself understanding. This is the link from Argh, suffering, to Oh, understanding.

Oh = understanding: fear and the four devils

Buddhism names four devils.

  1. The three poison consciousness: greed, hate, stupidity. See Lesson 4.
  2. Worry.
  3. Fear of death.
  4. Everyone else's four devils.
  • All fear comes from the same fear: fear of death.
  • We never say this fear out loud or talk about it. So the fear stays, and we never face it or understand it.

The course covers the four devils in a later class.

Oh = understanding: the three kinds form a circle

Modern research treats the three kinds as separate. In truth they form a circle. Each kind affects the other two, and they cannot be broken apart.

  1. Worry and stress
  2. Stored in the body
  3. Blockage
  4. Pressure on nerves: pain
  5. Blood slows
  6. Organs damaged
  • Stored worry creates blockage. Blockage presses on nerves and causes pain, such as a headache.
  • Blockage also slows the blood. When blood does not flow well, organs get damaged.
  • So pain and tension in the body is not only physical.

Oh = understanding: where to start

  • No single starting point fits everyone. Start with whichever kind is the cause.
  • In most cases, starting with the body helps most. Relaxing the body can stop the worry too, or at least help a lot.
  • A person full of worry often will not look after the body. The worry says "take care of my worry first", and the person handles worry by worrying more.
  • Do not argue with that person. If you go against the person, you lose their trust.
  1. Help with the worry first, or find something that works.
  2. Earn trust.
  3. Then work on the body.

Oh = understanding: passive side and active side

Passive side: clear out the bad
  • Mind: no more worry.
  • Psychology: no more stress.
  • Body: no more pain and tension.
Active side: build the good
  • Mind: sharp, positive, logical and not personal, with good ideas, wise intention and self-confidence.
  • Psychology: inner peace, freedom, relaxation and joy.
  • Body: harmony and balance. Yi Jin Jing and yoga help. Gym training helps too, if it builds balanced, working muscle: muscle linked to the whole body, free to move, with good posture. Gym training that builds tension and pain does not help.
  • The active side is hard to build until the passive side is cleared. Doing both is best.
  • When both sides happen, suffering does not only disappear. Joy appears.
  • With both sides, wisdom keeps growing, and we gain the power to be free from every kind of suffering.

Is this already releasing?

  • Tim and GD asked if this is already Mmm, releasing. Sifu said no. Releasing comes next.
  • Buddhism says wisdom frees us from suffering. "Wisdom" is a broad word, so Sifu adds the detail: which wisdom frees us from worry, from stress, and from pain and tension?

Why modern people need Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong

  • Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong has power for all three kinds of suffering.
  • The mantra helps the mind stop worry, helps psychology stop stress, and helps the body relax, which releases tension and pain.
  • How the mantra does this comes in a later class.
