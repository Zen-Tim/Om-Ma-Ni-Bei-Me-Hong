Lesson 6

Where suffering comes from: blind searching

More on Oh, the second sound of Om. Oh means understanding. Sifu shows where suffering starts: inside us, in blind searching for "I and mine". Then he shows why understanding is not yet ability, and how to practise.

Summary

The second sound of Om is Oh. Oh means understanding. Lesson 5 showed how to fix suffering. Lesson 6 shows where suffering comes from.

  • Oh = understanding, two timings: a temporary way gets rid of suffering for now. An eternal way pulls out the root, so suffering stops for good.
  • Oh = understanding, inside and outside: the root of suffering is inside, in our own blind consciousness. Outside is the body and the space around us.
  • Oh = understanding, blind searching: from birth, we all search for two things. "I": who and what am I? "Mine": what do I want to own? This searching keeps us in reincarnation, the cycle of being born again and again.
  • Oh = understanding, two kinds of searching: ego searching gives fun that fades. Curiosity searching develops wisdom, and its joy lasts. Curiosity searching is the only key out of suffering and reincarnation.
  • Understanding is not ability: reading a tennis book does not mean you can play tennis. Mmm, the third sound, is the trained ability to release suffering.

To practise, start by listening to your own voice as you sing the mantra. The higher level joins the mantra to open joints, charged energy and Buddhist breathing. That higher level leads to Nirvana, the fourth sound.

The four sounds of Om

Sifu ran through the four sounds again. This lesson stays on the second sound.

Sound 1

Argh

Suffering. Lesson 4.

Sound 2

Oh

Understanding where suffering comes from. Lessons 5 and 6.

Sound 3

Mmm

The ability to release suffering. Sifu calls it a healing ability.

Sound 4

Silence

Joy. Nirvana.

Oh and Mmm are different. Oh is knowing. Mmm is being able to do. See Understanding is not ability.

Oh = understanding: two timings

Oh is the wisdom to understand where suffering comes from. Sifu splits it by timing.

Temporary

How suffering appears now, and the wisdom that gets rid of it for now.

Eternal

The root that keeps creating suffering, and the wisdom that pulls out the root for good.

Lesson 5 called these the temporary fix and the permanent fix.

Oh = understanding: inside and outside

Is this wisdom only for yourself, or for others too?

GD asked if the wisdom is internal (inside you) or external (outside you), since you can also help someone else. Sifu: suffering comes from both.

Inside: the root
  • Our consciousness is not yet developed. It is blind.
  • Blind consciousness searches, because it wants to know what life is.
  • That searching power pulls us into reincarnation.
Outside: the body and space
  • In reincarnation, every blind consciousness together creates space and time.
  • What we experience is space and time, and that includes our body.
  • The body and the space around us are outside.

The body is a space made of four elements

ElementIn the body
AirBreath.
FireBody heat.
WaterMost of the body.
EarthThe solid part.
  • In open space, the four elements stay separate, though they still connect.
  • In a body, the four elements must unite in harmony.
  • The way the body grows also creates time.
Suffering comes from inside and outside. We have to wake up to both.

Tim recalled another way Sifu puts it: inward attitude and external action. Sifu agreed.

Oh = understanding: blind searching

What does "searching" mean?

GD asked: someone might say, "I don't search for anything. I'm just me." Sifu: everyone searches, from the day we are born, for two things. No one can stop.

  1. "I": who am I, what am I? What kind of being do I want to be? This is the first searching.
  2. "Mine": what do I want to own? What I do not have yet, and what I already have.
  • Our thinking power works on these two subjects most of the time.
  • Lessons 3 and 4 called this "I and mine".

Oh = understanding: what is wrong with searching?

GD: searching is fun. Why call it blind?

  • Searching is fun when you find what you want without suffering.
  • But the way we grow up and search brings much suffering. We search because there is so much blindness.
  • Even when it is fun, the fun disappears. When the fun goes, you are not happy and not satisfied. A negative side appears.

Buddhism compares this to honey on a knife. The honey tastes sweet, but licking it cuts the tongue.

Oh = understanding: two kinds of searching

Ego searching: the fun fades
  • Society teaches us to search for the ego, the "I".
  • Society teaches us to hold on to things and people: possessions and relationships.
  • The fun is temporary.
Curiosity searching: the joy lasts
  • Searching to understand life, and the truth beyond everything, with no more blindness.
  • Eternal wisdom is already in us. We still have to develop it, the way any ability grows with practice.
  • So the joy lasts forever.
  • We are not born with curiosity searching. Society teaches ego searching instead.
  • That is why people are still in reincarnation.
Curiosity searching is not only okay. It is the only key out of reincarnation and out of suffering. It is why we learn Om Mani Padme Hom.

Why Tim and GD keep asking

  • Viewers may wonder why Tim and GD keep digging. GD: if you do not understand from your own heart, you will not trust the teaching. Digging is the opposite of accepting blindly.
  • Tim: he has to rebuild the teaching for himself. Then Dharma gives the answer, and he comes back to it. If he takes the answer from someone without understanding it, he cannot use it, and he gets lost.
  • GD: school taught us to learn the answer for the test, get the mark, and move on. Here you must understand until your heart feels sure and at peace.
  • A level above that: explain it to someone else. If your heart and brain are not clear, the words do not come out right.

Sifu's view: a global awakening

  • Sifu feels a global awakening is coming.
  • People now take in huge amounts of information, much of it through AI. Sifu thanks AI for this.
  • So much information makes people feel weak, insecure and stressed. Blind belief starts to fade. People start to wonder: "What is life? How do I get rid of suffering?"
  • The only way to end that stress is to find a true answer. So the huge amount of information pushes everyone toward waking up.

Sifu calls this the joint karma awakening. Joint karma is the karma we all create together. Sifu sees this timing as positive.

Understanding is not ability

Where does understanding turn into healing?

Tim asked this. Sifu answered with tennis.

  1. Read a tennis book. You understand the rules, the court, and who serves first.
  2. That does not mean you can play tennis.
  3. To play well, go to the court, hold the racket and hit the ball. That is training, and training builds ability.
  • Sifu: when he plays tennis, his awareness cannot keep up with what he knows. He needs to train.
  • So Oh is understanding. Mmm is ability: the trained ability to release suffering.
  • GD's example: you leave a calm class feeling peaceful. Someone cuts you off in traffic, and you get angry. Using the wisdom in every moment is a further level, and that is what we practise.

Two abilities

Temporary

Release suffering now, any time you want. Temporary suffering comes in many forms: mental, psychological and physical.

Eternal

Stop creating suffering at the root. Develop eternal wisdom and live in it now. Observe creation with pure logic. Then you create no blindness.

Both abilities are in every human. Om Mani Padme Hom can wake up both.

Sifu: live the wisdom in each present moment, with no beginning and no end. Then the present becomes a present: a gift.

How to practise

Level 1: listen and enjoy

  1. Come to the class first. Understand the meaning, and see the benefit. Your psychology turns positive.
  2. As you say or sing Om Mani Padme Hom, use your mind to listen to the sound.
  3. Concentrate on the sound, and enjoy your own voice.

Now your mind and your psychology both join the mantra. Lesson 3 gave the same Level 1.

Higher level: two masters and the breath

Bodhidharma 達摩

Yi Jin Jing. Open every joint, with no tension.

Zhang San Feng 張三丰

The tai chi master. Charge the energy, let it run, and connect the whole body.

  1. Join both to the breathing that Buddhism teaches. Each breath charges energy and opens the joints.
  2. Sing Om Mani Padme Hom with that breath.
  3. Keep the brain fully on three things: the charge, the openness, and the sound. The brain stops its nonsense thinking.
  • Your whole consciousness wakes up, and unites with the body's subconsciousness: the awareness in the body that works below thinking.
  • Gravity pulls us into bad posture. Bad posture creates blockage, and blockage creates suffering. This practice works against gravity.
  • You lose physical pain and tension, and you wake up to your subconsciousness.
  • You can enter real still meditation, and feel complete content and joy. That is the fourth sound: Nirvana. Everyone can experience Nirvana.

Why the body matters

  1. Blocked joints, stuck energy
  2. Physical pain
  3. Mental worry and stress
  4. Down, negative psychology
  5. No joy, no spiritual energy

Bodhidharma brought Zen (Chan) Buddhism to China, and Yi Jin Jing is credited to him. Zhang San Feng is the Daoist master credited with founding tai chi.
