What you will learn

Four parts, one path

Each part of the mantra carries a meaning. Together the four parts map the way out of suffering.

嗡

Om

Four sounds: suffering, understanding, release from suffering, and a quiet, content heart.

嘛呢

Ma Ni

The wisdom jewel: your essence, which has no form, no birth and no end.

叭咪

Bei Me

The lotus: a heart that grows out of the mud of blind intention and comes out clean.

吽

Hong

Awakened consciousness. An awakened consciousness never goes back to blindness.

Start practising today

You do not need to understand everything first

Sifu's first practice works for everyone, from the first day.

  1. 1 Sing along with the sung version of the mantra. Singing holds your attention better than saying.
  2. 2 Hear every sound you sing. Enjoy listening to your own voice.
  3. 3 Sing 2 or 3 rounds. Do not count. Counting is more thinking.
