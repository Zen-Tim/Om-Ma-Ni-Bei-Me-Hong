Lesson 1 · 27 September 2026

The meaning

What each part of the mantra means, why you need no religion to use it, and why understanding beats blind belief.

Summary

Om Mani Padme Hom is a mantra in four parts: Om, Ma Ni, Bei Me and Hong. The four parts trace a path. You start in suffering. You understand life. You purify your heart. You wake up.

  • Om has four sounds: suffering, understanding, freedom from suffering, and a quiet, content heart.
  • Ma Ni is the wisdom jewel: your essence, which has no form, no birth and no end.
  • Bei Me is the lotus: a heart that grows out of the mud of blind intention and comes out clean.
  • Hong is awakened consciousness. An awakened consciousness never goes back to blindness.

You need no religion to use the mantra. The mantra is a training tool, a guide, a protection and a sound wave. The sound itself changes your heart, on top of the meaning.

Saying the mantra only to feel safe is blind belief, and blind belief does not remove fear. Understanding removes fear. First, understand your essence: your awareness is not the body or the "I" it is aware of. Second, understand that a perfect consciousness made the mantra out of pure kindness. That understanding gives wisdom trust, and wisdom trust gives real joy and safety.

The names

A note on spelling. Most English books write this mantra Oṃ Maṇi Padme Hūṃ, from Sanskrit. The common spelling in Chinese is 嗡嘛呢叭咪吽, Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong.

Sifu says the mantra the Chinese way. This course writes the mantra as Om Mani Padme Hom.

Om has four sounds

Sifu splits Om into four sounds. The four sounds follow in order.

Sound 1

Argh

Suffering.

Sound 2

Oh

Understanding. Not small things. Understanding life.

Sound 3

Mm

Free from suffering. Understanding life lets you help yourself and others out of suffering. Sifu calls this healing.

Sound 4

Silent

Content. Free from suffering, the heart is quiet, joyful and smiling.

Om has a second use. People say Om to begin meditation, to begin another mantra, or to bow to a Buddha or Bodhisattva. Used this way, Om means: "My heart is open and sincere. I want to learn complete wisdom and complete compassion."

Topics

Who Sifu is, and why study Dharma

Sifu has given his life to three questions. What is life? How do we get free from suffering? How do we get free of birth, aging, illness and death?

Sifu teaches healing, stretching, acupuncture, dry needling, Yi Jin Jing and tai chi. Sifu also teaches Buddhist philosophy, to give a complete view of life. The complete view of life is Dharma.

With a complete view of life, you stop acting from blind intention. With no blind intention, you stop creating blind suffering, for yourself and for others.

Many explanations

Sifu says the mantra has many explanations. Sifu does not say the others are wrong. The explanation Sifu teaches is not his own. Sifu learned it.

Sifu teaches this explanation for two reasons:

  1. The meaning and the function fit together perfectly.
  2. The explanation purifies Sifu's consciousness, and shows him the complete system of life.
Say Hong, not Om

The last word is a different word from the first. Om opens the mantra. Hong closes the mantra.

Sifu's memory aid: Hong is the feeling of waking up.

Do you need a religion to use the mantra?

No. Sifu's points:

  • In the Buddha's time, India had more than 1,000 religions. The Buddha tried to help people free themselves from religion.
  • The Buddha taught the true force of life: your awareness power. Without awareness power, you have no life.
  • Awareness power can be blind. Blind awareness power creates suffering, reincarnation, birth, aging, illness and death.
  • Your essence has no form, so your essence is already eternal.
  • The mantra helps you understand all of this. The mantra is open to everyone, with any religion or none.
Why the sound matters

Tim asked if the mantra is a training tool. Sifu agreed. The mantra is a training tool, a guide, a protection and a sound wave.

  • A noisy place makes you nervous.
  • Birdsong, gentle wind, ocean waves, a river or a waterfall make you feel joyful.

Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong is a sound wave made by a perfect consciousness. So the sound itself affects your heart, on top of the wisdom in its meaning.

Fear, blind belief and wisdom trust

GD asked about saying Om when you feel afraid, or in a nightmare. GD recalled that if you put your whole heart into saying Om, and think of nothing else, Om can calm your heart and even change the situation. Sifu said a deep wisdom sits behind the answer, and Sifu will explain it next time. Sifu answered GD's question in Lesson 2.

What does not work

Saying the mantra only to feel comfort or safety is blind belief. Blind belief is a wish. A wish does not mean you get what you wish for. The fear stays.

What works: two layers of understanding

When you understand the wisdom, you surely receive peace. You can also create peace yourself.

Layer 1: understand your essence.

  • Your awareness power is not the things you are aware of. Sifu: "A aware B, A is not B."
  • The mantra helps you understand your essence.
  • In meditation, you return to your essence. You stop connecting to the body.
  • When you stop connecting to the body, the "I" disappears. Any fear the "I" has disappears with the "I."
  • You then see life as a game: a journey to learn, share, develop and experience. You stop taking life too seriously.
  • Fear and worry come from taking this temporary character, the "I," too seriously.

Layer 2: understand where the mantra came from.

  • A perfect consciousness made the mantra.
  • That consciousness shared the mantra out of pure kindness, to help us.
  • When you understand that intention, you trust it.
  • That trust is not blind belief. That trust comes from understanding the wisdom. Sifu calls it wisdom trust.
  • An awakened consciousness has no ego. An awakened consciousness only wants to help and protect every life.
  • Understanding both layers gives you a deep protection, and real joy and safety.
Closing dedication

Sifu chanted Om, then led the class in this line. Repeat it at the end of practice:

May my heart share all the wisdom and compassion to every life.

How to make the mantra work

Sifu answers GD's question from Lesson 1: can saying the mantra remove fear and protect you?

Summary

GD asked in Lesson 1: can saying Om remove fear and protect you? Sifu's answer: a mantra works when you have two things.

  • Condition 1: understand the meaning.
  • Condition 2: have a sincere heart.

Either condition alone already works. Both conditions together surely work. Without either condition, saying the mantra is only a wish.

Condition 1: understand the meaning

Sifu ran through the four parts again, as one path.

Sifu: anyone curious about the meaning can go back to the first video. The first video is Lesson 1 on this page.

Condition 2: a sincere heart

  • A sincere heart begins when your heart sees that freedom from suffering is possible, and truly wants freedom from suffering.
  • Hold one heart and one intention.

"I truly want to wake up. I want to be out of blindness and suffering. I want to share the wisdom to help others."

  • Saying the mantra with that intention makes a connection.
  • Your heart feels peace right away.

Two sources of peace

Your own power

Their power
  • The awakened ones who made the mantra already have complete wisdom.
  • The awakened ones watch over you and want to help.
  • Knowing this makes you feel safe and free.

With both in mind, your heart becomes pure, joyful, peaceful and free as you say the mantra.

The meditation feeling

Sifu says a sincere heart takes you straight into the meditation feeling.

  • While you say the sounds, you stop thinking about yourself.
  • You feel the sound and the vibration, and you follow the meaning.

How to create a sincere heart

Tim asks how a student at home starts to practise. Sifu explains causation, then gives the first two levels of a sincere heart.

Summary

Tim asked: how does a student at home, who may never have heard the mantra, start to practise? Sifu first recapped Lesson 2. A mantra works when you have two things: you understand the meaning, and you have a sincere heart. Lesson 3 covers the sincere heart.

  • Causation: nothing comes from one cause. Many causes join into a result, and each result becomes a new cause.
  • Blind causation: normal life runs on "I and mine". This causation keeps us inside two systems: birth, aging, illness and death, and appear, develop, destroy and disappear. Going round again and again is reincarnation.
  • Spiritual causation: singing the mantra sincerely makes a different kind of causation. Spiritual causation comes from an eternal force of wisdom and compassion, not from "I and mine".
  • Level 1: listen to every sound you say or sing.
  • Level 2: wash, light incense, and bow to the statue of the Bodhisattva who made the mantra.

Sifu: you do not need to understand causation before you start to practise. Start with Level 1.

Causation

  • Causation means nothing happens without many things joining together.
  • One cause alone gives no result. One cause alone stays the same.
  • Many causes join together, and finally become a result.
  • The result then joins other causes as one cause, and makes a new result.
  • The chain never ends. We experience life through this chain.

Sifu's example: the river.

  1. Raindrops, ground water, mountain streams
  2. The river
  3. The ocean

One drop of water stays one drop. Many drops, plus water from under the ground and streams from the mountains, join into a river. The river is the result. The river then becomes a cause, and flows into the ocean with every other river.

Two systems that limit us

Normal life causation comes from trying to survive. Normal life causation looks at life from the ego: what is good for me, and what I want. Sifu calls this "I and mine". Our experience of life is limited by two systems.

Life-consciousness system

Consciousness connects to a body. The body goes through four stages.

  1. Birth
  2. Aging
  3. Illness
  4. Death
Space-and-time system

A consciousness in a body experiences space. Everything in space goes through four stages.

  1. Appear
  2. Develop
  3. Destroy
  4. Disappear
  • We have to follow both systems. Both systems run on blind, survival causation.
  • Reincarnation: blind causation pulls our consciousness through birth and death again and again.
  • Karma: causation that joins together and becomes the next lifetime.

Spiritual causation

  • Singing the mantra sincerely creates causation, but not normal life causation.
  • The mantra is an eternal force. The mantra does not come from "I and mine", and does not come from anyone's causation.
  • The mantra comes from a being with two qualities.
Wisdom

A completely open heart that understands life.

Compassion

Wanting to help others understand life, leave suffering, and feel joy without limit.

  • An awakened consciousness with this power made the mantra. When we learn the mantra, we move the same way.
  • When you are sincere, aware of the mantra and sing the mantra, you create Dharma spiritual causation right away.
  • Dharma spiritual causation is outside the survival cycle of birth, aging, illness and death. Dharma spiritual causation is not reincarnation causation.
  • Since causation that becomes the next lifetime is karma, Sifu also calls this spiritual karma. Singing or saying the mantra sincerely is all spiritual karma.

Tim and Sifu: you do not need to understand causation or the two systems right now. Take notes if you like, and start to practise. The sincere heart has the most direct effect.

Level 1: concentration

Level 1 of a sincere heart is concentration. Listen to what you say.

  • As you say or sing Om Mani Padme Hom, hear every sound exactly.
  • Listening stops the thinking, or at least stops nonsense thinking from controlling you.
  • Do not try to stop thinking. Sifu says Buddhism sees thinking as a river or a waterfall with no break. Wanting to stop thinking is like taking a knife to cut a waterfall. Wanting to stop thinking does not work.
  • Do not worry whether you say the mantra correctly. Worry is more thinking.
  • Do not try to hold everything you have learned about the mantra while you say the mantra. Holding every detail starts the thinking again.
  • Just enjoy listening to your own voice. If you enjoy listening, you have already reached Level 1.
Singing works better than saying. Singing has rhythm, melody and tone, and rhythm, melody and tone hold your attention even more.

Level 1 helps everyone, and connects you right away to the spiritual causation of the mantra.

Level 2: wash, incense, bow

Level 2 of a sincere heart: wash, light incense, and bow to the statue of Thousand Hands, Thousand Eyes Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva. The Chinese phrase for the first two steps is 沐浴焚香: bathe and burn incense, a way to show sincerity.

  1. Wash.
  2. Light incense.
  3. Bow to the statue.

Why this statue

Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva made the mantra. Thousand Hands, Thousand Eyes Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva is the same consciousness.

  • At the moment of becoming a Buddha, the Bodhisattva made a will to help every life.
  • The Bodhisattva observes every life, so a thousand eyes appear in the hands.
  • The Bodhisattva helps everyone, so a thousand hands appear.
Sifu's nameChineseCommon names
Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva觀世音菩薩Guanyin (Chinese), Avalokiteshvara (Sanskrit)
Thousand Hands, Thousand Eyes Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva千手千眼觀世音菩薩Thousand-armed Guanyin

The drought story

Many people in Chinese history saw Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva, often holding a vase of water. Sifu told one story. Sifu said the story has more than one version.

  1. Many places in China had no rain for a long time. Plants almost died. People had no food or water, and feared death. Nothing they tried worked.
  2. Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva appeared as an old lady, and told people to bow to Observing Life Sound Bodhisattva. Nobody understood or trusted the Bodhisattva yet.
  3. One person had heard a Buddhist describe the Bodhisattva as completely kind, completely caring, with an eternal heart that wants to help every life. That person was sure the Bodhisattva would help.
  4. That person sat in meditation and bowed under the sun for three days. Other people did not believe, and laughed.
  5. At the limit of what the person could bear, the person called out: "Please appear and show that your kind heart is real. I am truly sincere."
  6. The Bodhisattva appeared, and heavy rain covered the whole area. Many people saw the Bodhisattva. The Bodhisattva's form became the statue, and many more people came to know the Bodhisattva.

Stories like this happened many times, so the statue is very famous in China. The Bodhisattva created the mantra, so you bow to this statue.

A higher level, for later: Tim guessed that Level 2 is energy rising from the spring point (湧泉, a point on the sole of the foot), through the body, to Bai Hui (百會, the point on top of the head). Sifu said that is a much higher level, and too big a jump for Level 2.

Homework

Practise Level 1: listen to every sound as you sing.

  1. Sing along with the sung version of the mantra. Singing beats saying.
  2. Each sung round holds the mantra four times. Sing 2 or 3 rounds: 8 or 12 times in all. You do not need the full 25-minute session.
  3. Hear every sound you sing. Sifu says 8 or 12 times with every sound heard is already a high standard for Level 1.
  4. Do not count. Asking "Have I done 8, 9 or 12?" is more thinking, and the count does not matter.

What is suffering?

The first sound of Om is Argh, and Argh means suffering. This class explains suffering: the modern way, then three Buddhist ways. At the end, Sifu starts on the second sound, Oh: where suffering comes from.

Summary

The first sound of Om is Argh. Argh means suffering. This class explains suffering.

Lesson 2 gave two conditions for the mantra to work: understand the meaning, and have a sincere heart. Lesson 3 covered the sincere heart. Lesson 4 goes back to the meaning.

  • Argh = suffering, the modern way: suffering comes in three kinds. Physical suffering is pain. Mental suffering is worry. Psychological suffering, or stress, is fear.
  • Argh = suffering, the Buddhist way, part 1: birth, aging, illness and death. Each stage of the cycle is suffering.
  • Argh = suffering, the Buddhist way, part 2: blind birth and blind death. Where does birth come from? Something comes before the cycle. We do not know why we were born, or when we will die. A blind life leads to a blind next life, and the whole cycle repeats. That is reincarnation.
  • Argh = suffering, the Buddhist way, part 3: the three poison consciousness. We create our own desire for "I and mine", and that desire is greed. Greed creates comparing. Comparing becomes hate. Hate creates stupidity. We create every step ourselves.
  • The bridge to Oh, the second sound of Om: suffering is an effect, and every effect comes from a cause. Our own blind action creates the cause.

All of this is only the first sound of Om. Om is the first of six syllables in Om Mani Padme Hom. That shows how much wisdom sits inside this one mantra.

The four sounds of Om

A recap from Lesson 1. Om holds four sounds, in order. Together the four sounds trace the cycle of life, from suffering to emptiness.

Sound 1

Argh

Suffering. Say it strong and active, so it sounds like suffering.

Sound 2

Oh

Understanding suffering, and how to release it.

Sound 3

Mmm

Releasing. Like the sound after a great massage.

Sound 4

Silence

Empty, Nirvana, joy, content. All suffering released, back to emptiness.

Mmm in Buddhism is more than ordinary relief. "Argh, I hurt my hand," then the pain fades: that is human relief. Buddhist Mmm is complete freedom from suffering, and freedom from being trapped in reincarnation. The course reaches Mmm and Silence later.

Argh = suffering: the modern way

Three kinds of suffering.

Everyone knows the word suffering. Nobody looks at suffering as closely as Buddhist philosophy does.

Physical

Pain.

Mental

Worry.

Psychological

Stress, which we feel as fear.

This modern way is Sifu's own explanation. The Buddha did not say it this way. The modern way is still true, because it matches the Dharma.

The Buddha said everything he taught in his whole life was like a little dirt under a fingernail. Compare that dirt to the whole person: from the dirt alone, you cannot know who the person is. In the same way, what the Buddha taught is one little part of all Dharma. Everything the Buddha taught is true. So any new explanation is fine, as long as it matches what the Buddha taught.

Argh = suffering: the Buddhist way, part 1

Birth, aging, illness, death.

The first Buddhist way to see suffering is the cycle of four. Each stage is suffering in itself.

  1. Birth
  2. Aging
  3. Illness
  4. Death

Birth

  • Birth is suffering for the mum and for the baby.
  • A baby is sensitive. Everything about a baby is fragile and delicate.

Aging

Aging starts the day we start to grow up. Growing up means handling change, and change brings suffering.

The mind: comparing
  • Kids must learn many new things. Kids compare: who learns faster, who is stronger. Fall behind, and you feel stupid and afraid.
  • Adults compare too: a better job, more pay. Comparing is a constant stress.
  • There is always something to make us suffer.
The body: growing and wearing out
  • Teeth grow, and bones grow. Growing hurts.
  • Teenage years bring pimples.
  • Old age brings stiffness. Organs and nerves stop working. Some old people need diapers or a wheelchair, unless they learn wisdom to stay young and lively.

Habit: black hole or big bang

Old people get stuck in worry habits. We create our own habits, so we choose which way a habit grows.

Black hole

A habit in the wrong direction. Once you are in it, it grows bigger and bigger, and pulls you in further.

Big bang

A habit in the right direction. Open and expanding. It covers every part of life, and makes us healthier, happier and wiser.

The sharp student: wisdom can bring suffering too

  • A slow student learns to be sharp, to observe. Sharpness helps that student's life.
  • Sifu told one sharp student: you see the mistakes other people make. That is good. You are wiser than them.
  • But if you follow the sharpness everywhere, to everyone and everything, the side effect is anger and complaining. You feel bad. That is a black hole, and Sifu calls it blind habit.
  • The student did not want that, and chose to change.

Learning wisdom one time is not enough, because life is complex. Wisdom needs harmony and timing. Showing your sharpness in a kind, harmonious way is a separate wisdom, and not easy. Sifu says he has the same problem: his sharpness wants to help people learn, but everyone is at a different level, with a different character. Learning wisdom is a subject for the heart. We can spend our whole life on it.

Stress that never ends

  • When young, we think: "Once I finish this, life will be great." That day never comes.
  • There is always more to learn. Whatever we learn never feels enough, and we can never fall behind.
  • The mind starts to believe there is no end. People turn bitter and depressed, and lose joy in the future.
  • Many smiles hide this suffering. The smile is friendly, but inside is suffering.
A better view of life: enjoy what you have learned, and appreciate what you have learned. Stay humble too, because there is more to learn.

Illness and death

Too much suffering from birth and aging leads to illness. Worry and stress settle into the body and block it.

  1. Worry and stress
  2. Blockage in the body
  3. Nerves tense
  4. Blood cannot flow
  5. Organs fail
  6. Death
  • Someone who worries all the time gets bad skin, and breathing and blood flow problems.
  • Blood brings food to every organ, and clears the waste out. When blood cannot flow, organs cannot work or clean themselves, and they fail.
  • So aging finally becomes death.

The two ways join here. Birth, aging, illness and death are still physical, mental and psychological suffering. Sifu gave the detail to make that link clear.

Argh = suffering: the Buddhist way, part 2

Blind birth, blind death. Where does birth come from?

Part 1 looked at the cycle of birth, aging, illness and death. Part 2 asks what comes before the cycle. Birth has a cause, like everything else. Birth is not only mum's egg and dad's sperm. Consciousness has to come from somewhere, and we do not understand how we connect to our past life.

Blind birth

We are born into this human body without knowing how or why.

Blind death

We will surely die, without knowing when or how.

  • If this life happens blindly, how could the next life not happen blindly? No chance.
  • Life keeps happening blindly, and the whole cycle of birth, aging, illness and death repeats. That is reincarnation.
  • Om Mani Padme Hom holds the answer for how to clean this out.

Argh = suffering: the Buddhist way, part 3

The three poison consciousness: greed, hate, stupidity.

Buddhism has many more ways to explain suffering. Part 3 is the three poison consciousness. Each poison grows out of the one before, and we create every step ourselves.

  1. Our desire for I and mine
  2. Greed
  3. Comparing
  4. Hate
  5. Stupidity
  • We create our own desire for "I and mine". That desire is greed.
  • Greed creates comparing: what I have against what others have.
  • Comparing becomes hate.
  • Hate creates stupidity.

Greed

Greed brings suffering at every stage.

  1. Chasing it: suffering.
  2. Getting it, and finding it is not as good as you hoped: suffering.
  3. Fearing you will lose it: suffering.
  4. Fearing you cannot keep it: suffering.
  5. Seeing other people with something better, so yours is no longer good enough: suffering. Comparing makes greed grow.

Dealing with people

  • Dealing with people is suffering, because each of us has an ego: "I and mine".
  • When what we are is compared with other people, we suffer.
  • When other people want what we want, we compete, and we suffer.

Compassion, towards yourself and towards others, has two types: temporary and eternal. Temporary releasing has three parts.

  1. Mind: free from worry.
  2. Psychology: do not get trapped in stress. Do not follow negative emotion of any kind, or moody, negative feelings. This is very, very difficult.
  3. Body: make the body powerful. Curl the stomach to charge up the energy. Bring that charge up into a proud, open chest and open shoulders. The whole body connects as one piece, like a dragon twisting, with the breath. This takes the body out of the blindness of tension and suffering, into a powerful, healthy human body.

When you have the power to be free in all three, the Mmm of Om appears.

  1. Free in mind, psychology and body
  2. Mmm appears
  3. You enjoy life and help others
  4. Spiritual karma appears
  5. The heart is completely content
  6. Nirvana, the silence

Lesson 6 gave the same body work through Bodhidharma's Yi Jin Jing and Zhang San Feng's tai chi. Lesson 5 called the two types the temporary fix and the permanent fix.

A first look at Nirvana: the fourth sound

  • Nirvana is when your consciousness is completely free, no longer limited by the body.
  • You can be aware of any space and any time. You are aware that the true essence of life is already perfect.
  • Our true essence has the ability to create spiritual life and spiritual joy.
  • Because you make yourself free from blindness, you go back to your essence. There you find your essence is already perfect. That is natural Nirvana.

Nirvana becomes unlimited

  • Your own perfect, joyful essence creates spirit, light, joy, wisdom and compassion.
  • Then you help others reach Nirvana too. When they become free from suffering and reincarnation, they go back to their own essence, and experience Nirvana from their own essence.
  • You connect to their journey. You share it, and you are aware of it. Your awareness is no longer limited to your own perfect essence. It connects to the perfect essence of every life you help awaken.
The more you connect and the more you help, the more unlimited it becomes. That is unlimited joy through eternal wisdom.

Sifu's closing wish: may everyone be free from blindness, find their perfect essence, and go into unlimited joy.

The full view: EFG (essence, function, goal)

Sifu sums up the whole mantra from beginning to end. Om is the foundation, then Ma Ni, Bei Me and Hong. Then essence, function and goal, with three examples: a cup, a smartphone and a human.

Summary

Blindness exists, so awakening surely exists. Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong frees us from blindness and suffering.

  • Om: four sounds. Argh (suffering), Oh (understanding), Mmm (releasing), silence (content and perfect).
  • Two styles of awakening: temporary (take care of each case of suffering) and eternal (take out the root, the cause, of suffering).
  • Ma Ni: the wisdom jewel. Complete wisdom, never wrong for any space, any time and any life form.
  • Bei Me: the lotus. Compassion, the purifying power that grows something pure and beautiful out of muddy dirt.
  • Hong: the graduated consciousness. It already has the power to free itself and others. Hong equals Buddha.
  • Essence, function and goal: the mantra shows all three for life. The goal is never in the future. It is in each present moment, as a perfect gift.
  • Double awakening: your own understanding, plus connection to awakened consciousness.

Everything has an essence, a function and a goal. Looking for all three in daily life grows wisdom faster.

The full view in four points

Point 1: blindness means awakening exists

  • We are born blindly. We do not know why or how we suddenly became a human.
  • Then we follow the blind path: we grow, we age, we sometimes get sick, and we die.
  • If blindness exists, awakening surely exists. It is like a dream: if you are dreaming, you can surely wake up.
  • If there is suffering, there is surely a way to get rid of suffering. If there is blindness, wisdom surely exists to clean the blindness out.

Point 2: Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong frees us

The mantra helps us become free from blindness and free from suffering.

Point 3: the sound Om has four meanings

Sound 1

Argh

We blindly create suffering. Lesson 4.

Sound 2

Oh

We understand where suffering comes from. Lessons 5 and 6.

Sound 3

Mmm

We release suffering for ourselves and help others. Lesson 7.

Sound 4

Silence

We become content. All four together mean perfect.

Point 4: awakening has two styles

Temporary and eternal. The next two sections explain each one.

Temporary awakening: take care of each case

Temporary means you see the cause of one case of suffering, and you take care of that case.

Our suffering comes from birth, aging, illness and death. These four cover three kinds of suffering, and each kind has its own temporary fix.

Mind: worry

Learn not to follow the power of worry.

Psychology: stress

Purify the stress and become free from it.

Body: pain and tension

Relieve the pain and tension in the body.

The aim is to go back to pure joy, like a happy kid who lives in the present and enjoys life.

These are all temporary. In each case, you learn some wisdom straight away and take care of it.

Lesson 7 gave the same three parts of temporary releasing: mind, psychology and body.

Eternal awakening: take out the root

  • Our awareness power is our true self.
  • Right now that awareness power experiences life through the body. It connects to this universe, and to this space and time.
  • If our awareness is awakened, we will surely choose to create joy, not suffering. So we help our consciousness awaken.
  • We observe our consciousness, and see in which way it creates suffering.
  • When we take out the root (the cause), we no longer create suffering.
  • The other side: our awareness learns how to create joy.
  1. Observe your consciousness
  2. See how it creates suffering
  3. Take out the root
  4. The root becomes wisdom
The root is not only taken out. It is transcended into wisdom.

The whole process of Om also shows up in Ma Ni, Bei Me and Hong.

Ma Ni: the wisdom jewel

  • Ma Ni is like the Oh sound. It means wisdom.
  • In Sanskrit, Ma Ni means the wisdom jewel. A jewel is round. Round means complete. So Ma Ni means complete wisdom.
  • We normally do not understand what complete wisdom is. Complete wisdom has three powers, and it is never wrong.
Any space

Wherever you go, you are never outside this Dharma system.

Any time

At any time, the system keeps running perfectly the same.

Any life form

Every life form is inside this system.

Complete wisdom covers all three, eternally: a complete view of karma.

Lesson 1 described the Ma Ni jewel as the wisdom in your essence. The questions below link the two.

Bei Me: the lotus

  • Bei Me means compassion. It also means lotus: a purifying system.
  • A lotus grows in muddy dirt. It grows into something pure and beautiful, and it smells incredible.
  • That ability, turning muddy dirt into a pure, amazing lotus, shows the highest power of our consciousness.
  • How? Right now we are in blindness and suffering. Through observing, we can create wisdom and joy for ourselves, and share it with others.
  • From blindness and suffering, we can change and become joy and wisdom, like a lotus. That is our true spiritual ability.
  • Sifu links this back to Ma Ni: the wisdom jewel is what lets the lotus grow out of the mud.

True compassion is natural being

  • We cannot really "compassion" others. We can share our care and our kindness, in a natural way of being.
  • What does that mean? I do not aim my kindness at one special person. I just am a kind person, and I share that kind power with every life.
  • I always share it in a wise way: careful, with good intention, with joy.
  • True compassion is not pity. It is sharing a pure, kind heart and pure care, with no ego, in a wise way, with joy and no greedy intention, towards every life.
It is like the sun. The sun does not say, "I will shine my light only on this flower." It shines golden light on everything. Our heart can do that too.

Lesson 7 covered the ego kinds of compassion and the five conditions of true compassion.

Hong: the graduated consciousness

  • Hong, the last sound, means the consciousness has already graduated. It has already reached that power, and it is able to do it.
  • The wise jewel gives a complete, eternal view of life. What is it for? To help yourself and others become free from blindness and free from suffering.
  • When the consciousness has that ability, that is the last sound, Hong.
  • Tim's summary: Hong is when the consciousness can do this so perfectly that it is no longer limited by time, space or consciousness, because the wisdom is complete. Sifu agreed.
  • That awakening includes wisdom too: understanding the essence, function and goal of life, and using that understanding to purify all suffering and blindness, for you and for every life.
Hong is basically equal to Buddha: a completely awakened consciousness. When this ability works perfectly, it becomes Buddha.

EFG: essence, function, goal

Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong clearly gives us the essence, function and goal of life.

Essence

Our awareness power. Consciousness is our essence. It can create blindness, so it can experience suffering. The essence is the part that feels suffering.

Function

We create suffering, but our consciousness has the power to observe it, and to change blindness into wisdom.

Goal

Not only for ourselves. We become wisdom power and kindness power. We free ourselves, then share that power with every life.

The goal is in the present

  • The goal is never in the future.
  • Sifu wrote a song with this line in it: the final perfect goal is in each present moment, as a perfect gift.
  • It is a perfect gift because we all have the ability. But we still need to learn how to open that gift.
  • Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong helps us open up the present. It helps us understand the view of life, purify our intention and develop our wisdom.
So, hearing this sound, we can develop our wisdom and awaken.

The last point: double awakening

  • Buddhas created this sound. Why? To help us.
  • Through the sound, we connect to perfect awakened consciousness: Buddha. Buddha protects us, guides us and influences us to awaken.
Self-awakening

You understand the meaning. This comes from inside you.

Connection awakening

You respect the meaning, respect what Buddha created to help you, and sincerely ask for help. You connect to awakened consciousness, so it can help you. This comes from outside you.

Inside and outside together: double awakening.

Lesson 7 gave the same two ways: direct and connection. Lesson 2 gave the two conditions: understand the meaning and a sincere heart.

Questions and answers

Tim: is Ma Ni the wisdom we learn, or our essence, or both?

In week one we learned Ma Ni is the wisdom jewel, our essence, with no form, no beginning and no end. Now Ma Ni covers essence, function and goal. Is it the same thing?

  • Sifu: in week one we talked about the ability itself as our essence, not about what that ability learns.
  • The wisdom ability is our nature. It is our seed. It is our essence.
  • That seed grows until it is aware of EFG: essence, function and goal, all three. That is what the ability learns: it sees through the truth of all three.
  • The wisdom jewel gives a complete view of life: complete in view, eternal in time, unlimited in quality. So it surely covers essence, function and goal.
Tim: is it my essence, or the essence of every life?

Sifu: every life's essence.

  • Every life can develop wisdom, because the wisdom seed is already in every life.
  • We all have the wisdom ability, but we still have to grow it. Once it grows, it has the power to understand the essence, function and goal of life.
  • Even when we are in the blindness zone, the Buddha seed is still there. The wisdom jewel is still there, but not yet developed.
  • Cleaning out blindness is not cleaning out the essence. Even before you clean anything out, the essence is still there.
GD: so it has the potential to develop?

Sifu: yes. GD: it can create positive or negative. At the moment we create negative, by creating blindness. Then we learn the wisdom and develop it into an ability. That ability cleans out the blindness, and learns not to create it again, for ourselves and for others.

Tim: is Bei Me the purifier?

The wisdom jewel covers every angle. Is Bei Me the purifier system that actually purifies your consciousness and every other consciousness? So it is not just fancy words. It is a real ability, in every present moment, to let that wisdom shine for yourself and others.

Sifu: yes. It becomes an ability for yourself and others.

EFG: a cup and a smartphone

Tim: we brought in essence, function and goal quickly, and people at home may not know it. Sifu: everything has these three parts. Give me something.

  • Tim asked: would the cup's goal be to hold a liquid? Sifu: no. The glass itself has no goal. Holding or not holding does not matter to the glass.
  • The goal is never the material itself.
  • For a smartphone, the goals can be many: convenience, helping people be aware of more, and business.

Homework: use it in daily life

  • Tim: Sifu told us many years ago that if you use essence, function and goal to observe things, you develop wisdom faster.
  • Sifu: yes. We normally do not do this. We see one part and think we know the whole thing, and that creates blindness.

EFG: a human

Sifu: this one is more tricky, abstract and complicated.

Essence of a human

Consciousness, which is awareness ability, plus the body, which is made of the four elements.

ElementIn the body
AirYour breathing.
FireYour temperature.
WaterMost of your body.
EarthThe solid, material part.
  • The body, plus the power to be aware through the body: together, that is a human.
  • These are human-style four elements. The same four elements exist in other animals too.
  • Essence means what makes something happen: what it is made of.

Function of a human: two main ones

  1. Sharing: we share emotion. Our ability to share and communicate is number one on earth. That is why we can develop so many things through sharing.
  2. Thinking: our sharing reached the point where we created language. We use language to think. We organise information into knowledge and pass it down through the generations.

A possible function of a human: awakening

  • Humans have another possible function: awakening. That is why we help ourselves and everyone, and that is Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong.
  • Humans have the power to feel other people's suffering, so we can develop compassion. That is half of the spiritual path.
  • Humans also have the power to observe the system of life (life forms) and the system of the universe (space and time).
  • Through that, we try to understand what is most meaningful in all this, and the final direction we can reach: complete freedom.
All life looks for freedom. Humans have the power to reach it.

High tech does not awaken us

  • High tech makes life more convenient and easier, and lets us be aware of more things outside. But it does not help us be aware of our own essence.
  • Sifu's example: people use their phones a lot. We think we receive a lot of information, but our neck bends forward. We are not aware of our body, our consciousness, our energy or our posture. We are lost in outside things.
  • High tech could help us awaken if we used it the right way. But it was designed for making money, entertainment and communication. So for now, it does not go that way.
The smartphone is not going to awaken. We have to awaken: the consciousness, the power behind the life form, has to awaken.

Closing: consciousness is already eternal

  • Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong helps us come back to being aware of our consciousness, and to develop it in a wise, kind way.
  • That is how consciousness finally realises the essence of life: consciousness itself has no form, and it is eternal.
  • We do not have to search for the eternal. The body cannot be eternal. Consciousness already is.
  • We are only connected to this body. Our true awareness power has no form. So we can meditate out of the body and enjoy awareness of the unlimited universe.
  • The function is to learn meditation: concentrate on ourselves, not on outside things.
  • In meditation, we become free from our surroundings and free from this body. We become pure self again: pure consciousness.
Pure consciousness is free in every space. That is called complete awakening. Om Ma Ni Bei Me Hong helps us in that direction.
